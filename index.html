中文

本仓库用于集中存放各类应用协议文件，例如隐私政策、用户服务协议等。这些文件通过静态页面对外提供，供 App Store、各大应用市场及审核流程引用与查验。

English

This repository hosts legal and policy documents for mobile applications—such as privacy policies and terms of service—served as static pages for app store submissions and compliance review.
