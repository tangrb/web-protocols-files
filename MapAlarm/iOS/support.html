权限排查

定位权限

到达提醒需要将定位权限设为 「始终」：

  1. 打开 iPhone「设置」→「隐私与安全性」→「定位服务」→「地图闹钟」
  2. 选择「始终」
  3. 若曾选择「使用 App 期间」，闹钟在后台可能无法触发

仅「使用 App 期间」时，你仍可浏览列表与编辑闹钟，但已开启的闹钟可能无法在后台可靠提醒。App 列表顶部会提示可前往系统设置恢复。

通知权限

应用在后台时，主要靠系统通知提醒你。若关闭通知：

  1. 「设置」→「通知」→「地图闹钟」→ 打开「允许通知」
  2. 确认横幅 / 锁屏提醒已开启

拒绝通知不会阻止进入 App，但后台到达时你可能听不到提醒。

后台与触发限制

  • 到达提醒由系统在你进入设定区域时触发，不会为了提醒而一直在后台持续定位。
  • 应用在后台时，铃声 / 震动可能较短或受限；打开提醒页面后，才会持续响铃与震动。
  • 低电量模式、专注模式、飞行模式或定位信号较弱时，可能延迟或未触发。
  • 「稍后提醒」会尽量在约 5 分钟后再通知一次；若系统判断你已离开该区域，会自动取消。若你强行关闭了应用，下次打开时仍可能继续处理未完成的提醒。

静音与专注模式

  • 静音开关 / 响铃开关：打开提醒页面后会尽量正常播放铃声；部分机型仍可能受系统静音策略影响。
  • 专注模式 / 勿扰：可能屏蔽通知横幅与声音。请在专注设置中允许「地图闹钟」，或临时关闭专注模式后再试。

当前版本说明

当前版本全部功能可免费使用，未提供订阅或应用内购买，因此也没有恢复购买、自动续费或取消订阅。闹钟数据仅保存在本机，换机或重装后需重新添加。

数据与隐私

  • 闹钟（目的地、半径、时段等）仅保存在你的 iPhone 上，卸载应用后即删除。
  • 位置信息只在你的设备上用于到达提醒与地图显示，不会上传到我们的服务器。
  • 无广告，也不会用第三方工具统计或跨 App 追踪你。
  • 完整说明见 隐私政策。

Permissions

Location

Arrival alerts require location access set to Always:

  1. Open iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Map Alarm
  2. Choose Always
  3. If you previously chose While Using the App, alerts may not fire in the background

With While Using the App only, you can still browse the list and edit alarms, but enabled alarms may not alert reliably in the background. The app list may show a banner directing you to System Settings.

Notifications

When the app is in the background, system notifications are the main way you get alerted. If notifications are off:

  1. Settings → Notifications → Map Alarm → turn on Allow Notifications
  2. Confirm banners / Lock Screen alerts are enabled

Denying notifications does not block opening the app, but you may miss alerts when arrival happens in the background.

Background and Trigger Limits

  • Arrival alerts are triggered by the system when you enter a set area. The app does not keep continuous GPS running in the background just for alerts.
  • While the app is in the background, sound / vibration may be short or limited. Continuous ringing and vibration start after you open the alert screen.
  • Low Power Mode, Focus, Airplane Mode, or weak location signal may delay or prevent an alert.
  • “Remind Me Later” tries to notify again after about 5 minutes; if the system determines you have left the area, it cancels automatically. If you force-quit the app, unfinished alerts may still resume the next time you open it.

Silent Switch and Focus

  • Silent / Ring switch: After you open the alert screen, the app tries to play the ringtone normally; some devices may still follow system silent policies.
  • Focus / Do Not Disturb: May hide notification banners and sound. Allow Map Alarm in Focus settings, or temporarily turn Focus off and try again.

Current Version

All features in the current version are free. There is no subscription or in-app purchase, so there is no Restore Purchases, auto-renewal, or cancel-subscription flow. Alarm data is stored only on this device; after switching phones or reinstalling, you need to add alarms again.

Data and Privacy

  • Alarms (destination, radius, time window, and so on) are stored only on your iPhone and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Location is used on-device for arrival alerts and map display only—it is not uploaded to our servers.
  • No ads, and no third-party analytics or cross-app tracking.
