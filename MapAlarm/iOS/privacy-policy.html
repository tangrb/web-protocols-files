地图闹钟隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 27 日  |  最后更新：2026 年 8 月 28 日

欢迎使用地图闹钟（Map Alarm，以下简称「本应用」）。本应用由唐荣兵（以下简称「我们」）开发并运营，用于在地图上设定目的地，并在您进入目标区域时发出到达提醒。

我们重视您的隐私。本政策说明本应用会接触哪些信息、如何使用这些信息，以及您可行使哪些权利。请在使用本应用前仔细阅读。

简要说明（核心要点）：

  • 本应用不需要注册账号；
  • 您的闹钟数据和位置处理仅在您的 iPhone 本地完成，我们不会将它们上传到远程服务器；
  • 本应用不含广告，也不使用第三方统计或追踪工具；
  • 使用地图搜索或把地图选点显示为地址时，相关请求由系统发往 Apple，不经过我们的服务器。

一、适用范围

本隐私政策适用于您在 Apple App Store 下载并使用地图闹钟的全部功能，包括地图选点与搜索、到达提醒、闹钟管理与系统通知。

二、本应用会接触哪些信息

需要说明的是：本应用以本地运行为主。下文所称「接触」是指应用为实现功能而在您的设备上读取或临时使用相关信息，不代表我们会将这些信息发送或保存到我们的服务器。

信息类型 何时接触 用途 是否离开您的设备
精确位置 您授权定位后：浏览地图、计算距离、检测是否进入目标区域时 显示当前位置、设定目的地，并检测您是否到达 否，仅在设备本地处理；后台到达提醒由系统完成
闹钟与偏好设置 您创建、编辑闹钟或修改默认值时 保存目的地、半径、时段等配置，并记住您的偏好 否，保存在手机本地
地点搜索与地址显示 您搜索目的地或在地图上选点时 查找地点，并将选点位置显示为可读地址 由 iOS 发往 Apple，不经过我们的服务器

此外：

  • 本应用不会主动读取您的通讯录、相册、麦克风、相机或健康数据；
  • 若您的 iPhone 向 Apple 发送崩溃报告，该信息由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理，并非本应用主动收集。

三、系统权限说明

本应用仅在您使用相关功能时才会请求以下权限。您可随时在 iPhone「设置 → 隐私与安全性」中关闭授权；关闭后，对应功能将无法使用。

1. 定位权限

  • 为什么需要：在地图上显示当前位置、计算距离，并在您进入目标区域时发出到达提醒。
  • 我们如何使用：位置仅在您的手机上处理。后台到达提醒由系统在您进入设定区域时触发，不会持续向我们上报定位。
  • 您可以不授权吗：可以。若拒绝定位，到达提醒功能将无法使用。仅授权「使用 App 期间」时，后台提醒可能无法可靠触发。

2. 通知权限

  • 为什么需要：在应用处于后台时，通过系统通知提醒您已到达。
  • 我们如何使用：通知仅包含与闹钟相关的信息（如目的地名称），不会上传，也不用于营销。
  • 您可以不授权吗：可以。前台仍可响铃提醒，但后台到达时您可能收不到通知。

四、我们如何使用这些信息

我们仅在以下必要范围内使用上述信息：

  1. 在地图上设定目的地，并检测您是否到达；
  2. 保存并恢复您的闹钟配置与偏好；
  3. 通过系统地图服务完成地点搜索与地址显示；
  4. 保障应用正常运行并符合法律法规要求。

我们不会将您的信息用于广告推送，也不会出售您的个人信息。

五、信息的保存与删除

  • 保存在哪里：闹钟与偏好设置保存在您的 iPhone 本地。
  • 保存多久：在您删除对应闹钟或卸载本应用前，上述数据会一直保留。
  • 如何删除：可在应用内删除闹钟或修改设置；卸载本应用可清除所有本地保存的数据。
  • 关于位置：本应用不会把您的位置轨迹单独存成文件；到达检测由系统在您进入设定区域时完成。

六、是否会与第三方共享

我们不会向任何第三方出售或共享您的个人信息，以下情况除外：

  • 事先获得您的明确同意；
  • 法律法规、司法机关或政府主管部门的强制性要求。

本应用未接入任何第三方广告、数据统计或用户追踪服务，不会在其他 App 中追踪您，也不会弹出「允许 App 跟踪您」的系统授权请求。

七、信息安全

我们采取以下措施保护您的信息：

  • 闹钟与位置处理在您的手机本地完成，无需联网上传；
  • 后台到达检测由系统在您授权后触发；
  • 遵循 Apple 平台的安全规范，及时修复已知问题。

请您妥善保管自己的 iPhone，避免他人未经授权使用您的设备。

八、您的权利

您享有以下权利：

  • 查看：可在应用内查看已保存的闹钟及其位置、半径、时段等配置；
  • 修改与删除：可编辑或删除闹钟，或通过卸载应用清除本地数据；
  • 撤回授权：可在 iPhone「设置 → 隐私与安全性」中关闭定位或通知权限；
  • 咨询与投诉：如对本政策有疑问或认为权益受到侵害，请通过文末邮箱联系我们。

我们将在收到您的邮件后 15 个工作日内回复。

九、未成年人保护

本应用面向一般用户。若您未满 18 周岁，请在监护人指导下阅读本政策并使用本应用。 我们不会主动收集未满 14 周岁儿童的个人信息；如监护人发现相关情况，请联系我们，我们将尽快处理。

十、App Store 数据收集说明

Apple 要求开发者在 App Store 页面公示应用的数据处理方式。本应用的实际情况如下，供您参考：

  • 不追踪您：本应用不会在其他公司的 App 或网站中追踪您的活动；
  • 不上传至开发者服务器：您的位置和闹钟数据不会发送到我们的服务器；
  • 无第三方服务：未使用广告、数据分析或追踪类第三方工具；
  • 权限用途明确：定位和通知权限仅用于上述功能，不做其他用途。

App Store 页面上的隐私标签以 App Store Connect 中的填写为准；如有出入，以本政策正文为准。

十一、政策更新

我们可能适时更新本政策。更新后会在本页面发布，并修改文首的「最后更新」日期。 若变更会显著影响您的权利，我们将通过应用内提示等方式告知您。 更新后继续使用本应用，即表示您同意更新后的政策。

Welcome to Map Alarm (the "App"). The App is developed and operated by Tang Rongbing ("we," "us," or "our"). It lets you set destinations on a map and alerts you when you enter the target area.

We value your privacy. This policy explains what information the App may access, how we use it, and what rights you have. Please read it carefully before using the App.

Summary (Key Points):

  • The App does not require account registration;
  • Your alarm data and location processing happen locally on your iPhone only—we do not upload them to remote servers;
  • The App contains no ads and does not use third-party analytics or tracking tools;
  • When you search for places or display a map pin as an address, those requests are sent by the system to Apple—not through our servers.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all features of Map Alarm that you download from the Apple App Store and use, including map picking and search, arrival alerts, alarm management, and system notifications.

2. Information the App May Access

Please note: the App operates primarily locally on your device. "Access" in this policy means the App reads or temporarily uses relevant information on your device to provide its features—it does not mean we send or store that information on our servers.

Information Type When Accessed Purpose Leaves Your Device?
Precise location After you grant location access: browsing the map, calculating distances, and detecting entry into a target area Show your current location, set destinations, and detect whether you have arrived No—processed locally on your device; background arrival alerts are handled by the system
Alarms and preferences When you create or edit an alarm, or change defaults Save destination, radius, time window, and other settings, and remember your preferences No—stored locally on your phone
Place search and address display When you search for a destination or pick a point on the map Find places and show a readable address for the selected location Sent by iOS to Apple—not through our servers

In addition:

  • The App does not proactively access your contacts, photo library, microphone, camera, or health data;
  • If your iPhone sends crash reports to Apple, that information is handled under Apple's privacy policy—it is not actively collected by the App.

3. System Permissions

The App requests the following permissions only when you use the related features. You may revoke authorization at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security; after revocation, the corresponding feature will be unavailable.

3.1 Location Access

  • Why needed: To show your current location on the map, calculate distances, and alert you when you enter a target area.
  • How we use it: Location is processed locally on your phone. Background arrival alerts are triggered by the system when you enter a set area—we do not continuously report your location to us.
  • Can you decline? Yes. If you deny location access, arrival alerts cannot work. With "While Using the App" only, background alerts may not fire reliably.

3.2 Notifications

  • Why needed: To alert you with a system notification when arrival happens while the App is in the background.
  • How we use it: Notifications contain only alarm-related information (such as the destination name). They are not uploaded and are not used for marketing.
  • Can you decline? Yes. Alerts can still play in the foreground, but you may not receive a notification when arrival happens in the background.

4. How We Use This Information

We use the above information only as necessary to:

  1. Set destinations on the map and detect whether you have arrived;
  2. Save and restore your alarm configuration and preferences;
  3. Complete place search and address display via system map services;
  4. Ensure the App operates properly and complies with applicable laws and regulations.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

5. Storage and Deletion

  • Where stored: Alarms and preferences are saved locally on your iPhone.
  • How long: Local data remains until you delete the corresponding alarm or uninstall the App.
  • How to delete: Delete alarms or change settings in the App; uninstall the App to clear all locally stored data.
  • About location: The App does not save your location trail as a separate file; arrival detection is handled by the system when you enter a set area.

6. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell or share your personal information with any third party, except:

  • When we have obtained your explicit consent in advance;
  • When required by laws, regulations, judicial authorities, or government agencies.

The App does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or user tracking services. It does not track you across other apps and will not prompt the system "Allow App to Track" authorization request.

7. Information Security

We take the following measures to protect your information:

  • Alarm and location processing is completed locally on your phone—no network upload required;
  • Background arrival detection is triggered by the system only after you grant permission;
  • We follow Apple platform security guidelines and promptly address known issues.

Please keep your iPhone secure and prevent unauthorized use of your device by others.

8. Your Rights

You have the following rights:

  • View: View saved alarms and their location, radius, time window, and other settings in the App;
  • Modify and delete: Edit or delete alarms, or clear local data by uninstalling the App;
  • Revoke authorization: Disable location or notification permissions in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security;
  • Inquiries and complaints: If you have questions about this policy or believe your rights have been affected, contact us via the email at the end of this document.

We will respond within 15 business days of receiving your email.

9. Protection of Minors

The App is intended for general users. If you are under 18, please read this policy and use the App under the guidance of a guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14; if a guardian discovers such a situation, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

10. App Store Data Collection Disclosure

Apple requires developers to disclose how apps handle data on App Store pages. The actual practices of this App are as follows:

  • No tracking: The App does not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites;
  • No upload to developer servers: Your location and alarm data are not sent to our servers;
  • No third-party services: No advertising, analytics, or tracking third-party tools are used;
  • Clear permission purposes: Location and notification permissions are used only for the features described above.

Privacy labels on the App Store page are based on App Store Connect submissions; if there is any discrepancy, this policy shall prevail.

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date at the top. If changes materially affect your rights, we will notify you through in-app notices or other means. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
