地图闹钟隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 27 日  |  最后更新：2026 年 8 月 27 日

欢迎使用地图闹钟（英文名 Map Alarm，以下简称「本应用」）。本应用由唐荣兵（以下简称「我们」）开发并运营，用于在地图上设定目的地，并在您进入目标区域时通过铃声、震动与通知进行到达提醒。

我们重视您的隐私。本政策说明本应用会接触哪些信息、如何使用这些信息，以及您可行使哪些权利。请在使用本应用前仔细阅读。

简要说明（核心要点）：

  • 本应用不需要注册账号；
  • 闹钟数据与位置处理仅在您的设备本地完成，我们没有自建服务器，也不会将它们上传到我们的服务器；
  • 本应用不含广告，也不使用第三方统计或追踪工具；
  • 当前版本不提供 App 内购买或订阅，全部功能可免费使用；地图搜索与地理编码由 Apple 系统服务完成。

一、适用范围

本隐私政策适用于您在 Apple App Store 下载并使用地图闹钟的全部功能，包括地图选点与搜索、地理围栏到达提醒、时间段限定、一次性/重复闹钟、列表距离显示、稍后提醒与本地通知。当前版本未开放 StoreKit 订阅或任何 App 内购买，您无需付费即可使用上述功能。若日后开通付费订阅，我们将更新本政策并在应用内告知。

三、系统权限说明

本应用仅在您使用相关功能时才会请求以下权限。您可随时在 iPhone「设置 → 隐私与安全性」中关闭授权；关闭后，对应功能将无法使用或可靠性下降。

1. 定位权限（使用期间 / 始终）

  • 为什么需要：在地图上显示当前位置、搜索附近目的地、计算到各闹钟的直线距离，以及通过系统地理围栏在您进入目标区域时触发提醒。
  • 我们如何使用：位置仅在设备上处理。到达提醒依赖系统地理围栏，需要将定位权限设为「始终」，才能在应用关闭时仍由系统检测是否到达。前台列表打开时，会短暂使用连续定位以刷新距离显示；后台不会持续开启标准 GPS 更新。
  • 您可以不授权吗：可以。仅授权「使用 App 期间」时，仍可浏览列表与编辑闹钟，但后台到达提醒可能无法可靠触发。若完全拒绝定位，核心到达提醒功能将无法使用。

2. 通知权限

  • 为什么需要：当应用在后台或被系统唤醒时，通过系统本地通知提醒您已到达目的地；「稍后提醒」也会使用本地通知。
  • 我们如何使用：通知内容仅包含与您闹钟相关的信息（例如目的地名称），不经由第三方推送通道，也不会用于营销。
  • 您可以不授权吗：可以。拒绝通知不会阻止进入应用；前台仍可全屏响铃提醒，但后台到达时您可能听不到提醒。

四、我们如何使用这些信息

我们仅在以下必要范围内使用上述信息：

  1. 在地图上设定目的地，并显示触发半径；
  2. 通过地理围栏检测您是否进入目标区域，并在符合时段等条件时发出提醒；
  3. 在闹钟列表中显示到各目的地的直线距离；
  4. 保存您的闹钟配置与新建闹钟默认值；
  5. 保障应用正常运行并符合法律法规要求。

我们不会将您的信息用于广告推送或用户画像，也不会出售您的个人信息。

五、信息的保存与删除

  • 保存在哪里：闹钟数据保存在您设备上的本机数据库中；偏好设置保存在系统 UserDefaults。上述数据不同步 iCloud，换机后需重新添加闹钟。
  • 保存多久：在您删除对应闹钟或卸载本应用前，上述本地数据会一直保留。
  • 如何删除：可在应用内逐条删除闹钟；在设置中修改新建闹钟默认值；卸载本应用可清除全部本地数据。
  • 关于付费：当前版本未开放订阅或 App 内购买，不会向您扣费，也不收集支付信息。

六、是否会与第三方共享

我们不会向任何第三方出售或共享您的个人信息，以下情况除外：

  • 事先获得您的明确同意；
  • 法律法规、司法机关或政府主管部门的强制性要求。

本应用未接入任何第三方广告、数据统计或用户追踪服务，不会在其他 App 中追踪您，也不会弹出「允许 App 跟踪您」的系统授权请求。本应用无自有服务器，因此也不存在由我们发起的跨境传输。

以下系统能力由 Apple 提供，相关处理受 Apple 隐私政策 约束：

  • MapKit / 地图与地点搜索：加载地图、搜索目的地时，系统可能向 Apple 发送请求；
  • 地理编码：将坐标转换为地址描述时，系统可能向 Apple 发送坐标。

Welcome to Map Alarm (the "App"). The App is developed and operated by Tang Rongbing ("we," "us," or "our"). It lets you set destinations on a map and alerts you with sound, vibration, and notifications when you enter the target area.

We value your privacy. This policy explains what information the App may access, how we use it, and what rights you have. Please read it carefully before using the App.

Summary (Key Points):

  • The App does not require account registration;
  • Alarm data and location processing happen locally on your device—we have no backend servers and do not upload this data to servers we operate;
  • The App contains no ads and does not use third-party analytics or tracking tools;
  • The current version does not offer in-app purchases or subscriptions—all features are free to use; map search and geocoding are provided by Apple system services.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to all features of Map Alarm that you download from the Apple App Store and use, including map picking and search, geofence arrival alerts, time windows, one-time/repeating alarms, list distance display, snooze, and local notifications. The current version does not offer StoreKit subscriptions or any in-app purchases; you can use these features without paying. If paid subscriptions are enabled later, we will update this policy and notify you in the App.

2. Information the App May Access

Please note: the App operates primarily locally on your device. "Access" in this policy means the App reads or temporarily uses relevant information on your device to provide its features—it does not mean we send or store that information on our servers. Precise location is sensitive personal information and is processed only after you grant permission, and only as needed to provide arrival alerts.

3. System Permissions

The App requests the following permissions only when you use the related features. You may revoke authorization at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security; after revocation, the corresponding feature will be unavailable or less reliable.

3.1 Location (When In Use / Always)

  • Why needed: To show your current location on the map, search nearby destinations, calculate straight-line distance to each alarm, and trigger alerts via system geofencing when you enter a target area.
  • How we use it: Location is processed on device only. Arrival alerts rely on system geofencing and need Location set to Always so the system can detect arrival even when the App is closed. Continuous location is used briefly while the alarm list is open to refresh distances; the App does not keep standard GPS updates running in the background.
  • Can you decline? Yes. With "While Using the App" only, you can still browse the list and edit alarms, but background arrival alerts may not fire reliably. If you deny location entirely, the core arrival-alert feature cannot work.

3.2 Notifications

  • Why needed: When the App is in the background or woken by the system, local notifications alert you that you have arrived; snooze also uses local notifications.
  • How we use it: Notification content relates only to your alarms (for example, the destination name). It does not go through a third-party push channel and is not used for marketing.
  • Can you decline? Yes. Declining notifications does not block you from using the App. Full-screen alerts can still play in the foreground, but you may not hear an alert when arrival happens in the background.

4. How We Use This Information

We use the above information only as necessary to:

  1. Set destinations on the map and display the trigger radius;
  2. Detect whether you enter a target area via geofencing and alert you when time-window and other conditions are met;
  3. Show straight-line distance to each destination in the alarm list;
  4. Save your alarm configuration and defaults for new alarms;
  5. Ensure the App operates properly and complies with applicable laws and regulations.

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell your personal information.

5. Storage and Deletion

  • Where stored: Alarm data is stored in an on-device database; preferences are stored in UserDefaults. This data is not synced to iCloud; after switching devices you must re-add alarms.
  • How long: Local data remains until you delete the corresponding alarm or uninstall the App.
  • How to delete: Delete alarms one by one in the App; change defaults in Settings; uninstall the App to clear all locally stored data.
  • About payments: The current version does not offer subscriptions or in-app purchases. We do not charge you and do not collect payment information.

6. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell or share your personal information with any third party, except:

  • When we have obtained your explicit consent in advance;
  • When required by laws, regulations, judicial authorities, or government agencies.

The App does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or user tracking services. It does not track you across other apps and will not prompt the system "Allow App to Track" authorization request. The App has no backend servers of our own, so we do not ourselves transfer data across borders.

The following system capabilities are provided by Apple and are subject to the Apple Privacy Policy:

  • MapKit / maps and place search: When loading maps or searching for a destination, the system may send requests to Apple;
  • Geocoding: When converting coordinates into an address, the system may send coordinates to Apple.

7. Information Security

We take the following measures to protect your information:

  • Alarm and location processing is completed locally on your device—no upload to servers we operate;
  • Background arrival detection uses system geofencing rather than continuously reporting a location trail;
  • The current version does not initiate StoreKit purchases or read subscription entitlements;
  • We follow Apple platform security development guidelines and promptly address known issues.

Please keep your iPhone secure and prevent unauthorized use of your device by others.
