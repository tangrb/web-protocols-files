2. Service

The App is the companion app for Hercuddle smart devices (such as Vionne) and currently provides:

  • Device connection: add your device over Bluetooth, view its connection status and the battery level of the device and its charging case;
  • My devices: view device details and session history, rename a device (the name is saved only in the App), and delete a device;
  • Custom Mode: No Limits, Shortcut, and Quadrants modes with built-in waveforms and adjustable intensity;
  • Music Sync: play built-in demo songs or audio files you import from your phone, with synchronized haptic patterns;
  • Usage Report: review your past sessions and replay how waveforms and intensity changed;
  • Others: contact us by email and read this Agreement and the Privacy Policy.

After you add a device, the App remembers it and automatically reconnects to it when the App is open and the device is nearby. To stop this, turn off the device, turn off Bluetooth, or delete the device in the App.

Features marked "Coming soon" (such as AI Mode) are not yet available. We may add, change, suspend, or discontinue features, and will notify you in the App when changes materially affect you.

Your device list, settings, usage records, and imported music are stored only on your phone. After switching phones or uninstalling the App, this data may not be recoverable unless it is included in your own device backup.
